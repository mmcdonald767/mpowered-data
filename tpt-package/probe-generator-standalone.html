Probe Generator

Define your parameters below and let AI generate a fresh 250–300 word narrative reading probe from scratch — then review, edit, format, and print a two-page packet: a clean student copy and a separate teacher scoring page.

Local-Only This tool runs entirely in your browser. No student data is ever collected — probe text is generated from curriculum parameters only, sent straight to Google's Gemini API using your own key, and nothing is stored or transmitted anywhere else.
